# 🧠 CalmSync AI – Transforming Stress into Calm Experiences

## 📌 Project Overview

CalmSync AI is a user-centered wellness application designed to transform everyday stress into a calm, immersive, and emotionally balanced experience.

This project applies **Design Thinking principles** to understand stress across different environments and provide personalized solutions using **AI, AR, and VR technologies**.

---

## 🎯 Objective

To reduce stress and improve mental well-being by providing:

* Real-time stress tracking
* Personalized AI-based relaxation
* Immersive VR/AR calming experiences
* Instant stress relief tools

---

## 🧠 Design Thinking Process

### 1. Empathize

Conducted research and identified stress triggers across environments:

* Academic pressure (students)
* Workload and burnout (professionals)
* Social anxiety (public environments)
* Sensory overload (crowded places)

**Common Emotions Identified:**
Anxiety, pressure, fatigue, frustration

## 🌌 Calm Pod Experience

The **Calm Pod** is the core feature of the app.

It provides:

* Environment selection (Ocean 🌊, Forest 🌿, Rain 🌧️, Space 🌌)
* Light, sound, and aroma controls
* Personalized relaxation settings
* Access to VR & AR modes

---

## 🌬️ Breathing Feature

A quick stress-relief tool that includes:

* Guided breathing animation
* Timer-based sessions
* Micro-relaxation support

Accessible both independently and inside Calm Pod.
